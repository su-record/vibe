# Release note from the changelog

## Why
A release note is read by people who will not open the changelog: one line per change, nothing
invented, nothing left as a placeholder, short enough to read in under a minute.

## What counts as success
- out/note.md exists, opening with a heading naming the release (80 characters or fewer).
- One line per changelog entry, twelve lines total or fewer.
- No placeholder text.
- Every number in the note is traceable to CHANGELOG.md.
