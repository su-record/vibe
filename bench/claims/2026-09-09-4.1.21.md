# Pre-registered claim — 4.1.21, run on 2026-09-09

Written before the bench runs; not adjusted after seeing the data. Arms: claude × {on, off}, codex × {on, off}; five runs per arm per task; `--parallel 3`; both arms isolated; the judge counts the task's scenarios only.

- Overhead set (settlement, vibe-fix, report): `on` checks not worse than `off`; `on` turns ≤ `off` turns + 4; `on` ms ≤ `off` ms × 1.5 — per client, means over the five runs (`checks/bench-gate.js`).
- Direction set (hidden-requirement, regression-trap, long-context, ambiguous-brief): per client, `on` scores higher on checks (`--metric checks`, `difference-observed`) for at least two of the four; a task where it does not is named as one the bare model already gets right and retired from the set.
- Whatever falls: the numbers are reported as measured.

## Result (2026-09-09, 140 runs, both arms isolated)
Overhead set, `on` vs `off` (mean turns · mean seconds; 4.1.20's clean numbers in brackets):
- claude settlement 12.0 vs 5.4 [18.8 vs 6.0] · 79 s vs 59 s (inconclusive) — **over the +4 allowance**
- claude vibe-fix 6.8 vs 3.6 [11.0 vs 3.2] · 25 s vs 17 s — within
- claude report 7.6 vs 3.2 [16.0 vs 3.0] · 31 s vs 21 s — **over the +4 allowance**
- codex settlement 20.6 vs 6.8 [31.8 vs 7.0] · 133 s vs 63 s — **over**
- codex vibe-fix 10.6 vs 7.0 [16.2 vs 6.8] · 34 s vs 28 s (inconclusive) — within
- codex report 7.2 vs 6.0 [19.8 vs 6.0] · 41 s vs 26 s — within
Quality: every arm 100% on every overhead task. Cost, claude: settlement $0.45 vs $0.31 · vibe-fix $0.22 vs $0.17 · report $0.24 vs $0.17.
Reading: the fast path cut the harness's own procedure by a third to a half (report on claude: 16.0 → 7.6 turns; codex: 19.8 → 7.2), but settlement and claude's report still exceed the +4 rule. What remains is not re-drafting or repeated checks any more; it is the `on` arm reading the intent, running `vibe state`, and one `check --all`, plus the model's own extra care once it sees an intent with five scenarios. The gate names settlement and report.

Direction set: **the bare model got every direction task right** — hidden-requirement, regression-trap, long-context 100% in both arms for both clients; ambiguous-brief 100% `on`, claude `off` 4,4,4,5,5 of 5 (inconclusive by range), codex `off` 100%. Per the pre-registration, all four are named as tasks the bare model already gets right and retired from the direction set; the harness prevented nothing here because there was nothing to prevent. A direction set that separates the arms needs traps a frontier model does not read its way out of in one pass — the numbers say a single hidden file, a shared helper and forty small modules are not that.

No saving is claimed for 4.1.21. What is claimed: the overhead fell from 2–5× to roughly 1.2–2.2× the bare model's turns on the same tasks, quality unchanged.
