# Pre-registered claim — 4.1.22, fifth run, 2026-09-09

Written before the bench runs; not adjusted after seeing the data. The user's rule the same day: no version split — everything known goes into 4.1.22, one bench, one deploy; the fourth run's "no further cut" is superseded by that decision and recorded here. What changed since the fourth run: the Claude `SessionStart` hook hands over `vibe state` (the first command, free); a `Stop` hook runs the verdict when a turn ends with an intent still building; `size: full` means the build skill applies, not a count of five; card rule 8 delegates only files over 400 lines and samples over 100 rows (Codex's transcripts showed `vibe read --ask` and `vibe profile` on a few hundred bytes); the `ask` task with a fake user; `session-split` at eight subcommands cut at a third. The gate now measures what the user measures — tokens and time; turns are reported. The 1.25 was chosen with the fourth run's 1.28–1.57 known: a target above it, not a fit.

- Overhead set (settlement, vibe-fix, report): `on` checks not worse; `on` weighted input tokens ≤ `off` × 1.25; `on` ms ≤ `off` × 1.5 — per client, means over five runs. Turns reported.
- Direction set: `ask` — per client, `on` scores higher on checks than `off` (`asked` in the ledger says who asked); `session-split` — per client, `on` checks not worse and `on` weighted tokens over the two sessions ≤ `off`.
- Context set (brownfield): `on` weighted tokens ≤ `off` × 0.7 per client (met on claude in the third run, not on codex).
- Whatever falls: the numbers are reported as measured; README's opening is written from them; the release ships with them.
