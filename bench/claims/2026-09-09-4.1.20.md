# Pre-registered claim — 4.1.20, run on 2026-09-09

Written before the bench runs; not adjusted after seeing the data.

- Tasks: settlement, vibe-fix, report. Arms: claude × {on, off}, codex × {on, off}. Five runs per arm per task, `--parallel 4`, `--max-turns 40`.
- Quality claim: `vibe ledger compare --by harness --metric checks --ledger bench/ledger.jsonl` must not show `on` worse than `off` (a `difference-observed` with a negative delta for `on` falsifies this release's premise).
- Efficiency claim: with `--paired`, `--metric turns` and `--metric ms` show `difference-observed` with `on` lower. If either lands `inconclusive` or `insufficient-runs`, no saving is claimed for 4.1.20 and the number is reported as it is.
- The ledger from earlier releases is moved aside (`bench/ledger.before-4.1.20.jsonl`) so every line compared here comes from this build.

## Amendment before the rerun (same day)
The first 60 runs are set aside as `bench/ledger.contaminated-2026-09-09.jsonl`: the `off` arm could see this machine's global vibe plugin (its agents recorded regressions with `vibe regress record`), and the judge counted those regressions as checks. Both arms now run under an isolated home and the judge counts the task's scenarios only. The claims above are unchanged.

## Result (2026-09-09, 60 clean runs, both arms isolated)
- Quality: every arm passed every judge scenario in every run (settlement 5/5, vibe-fix 1/1, report 4/4). `--metric checks` is `inconclusive` everywhere; the gate passes. The tasks are saturated: they do not separate the arms on quality.
- Efficiency: the pre-registered claim is **falsified** on this task set. `--paired` verdicts, `on` vs `off` (mean turns · mean seconds):
  - claude settlement 18.8 vs 6.0 turns (difference-observed, on higher) · 88 s vs 66 s (inconclusive)
  - claude vibe-fix 11.0 vs 3.2 · 27 s vs 14 s (both difference-observed, on higher)
  - claude report 16.0 vs 3.0 · 49 s vs 15 s (both difference-observed, on higher)
  - codex settlement 31.8 vs 7.0 · 168 s vs 62 s; vibe-fix 16.2 vs 6.8 · 58 s vs 24 s; report 19.8 vs 6.0 · 76 s vs 30 s (all difference-observed, on higher)
  - claude cost, reported: settlement $0.57 vs $0.34 · vibe-fix $0.28 vs $0.16 · report $0.40 vs $0.15
- Reading: on a task the bare model gets right in 3–7 turns, the harness adds 8–25 turns of its own procedure (state, skills, checks, a regression recorded for the fixed bug) and no quality. The first, contaminated run had shown the opposite only because the `off` arm was slowed by the operator's global plugin. No saving is claimed for 4.1.20.
- What the bench must change: tasks the bare model fails (an ambiguous brief, a hidden requirement, a multi-file change that regresses something) and tasks long enough for rediscovery to matter; saturated tasks are retired, as the spec says. What the harness must change: its per-task overhead is the number to cut before any saving can be measured.
