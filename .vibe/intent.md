# vibe 4 · 4.1.11 — antislop reaches design, and the text packs take what ten public anti-slop skills got right

## Why
The antislop family strips what a model produces by default when nobody decided otherwise. So far it covers prose: `antislop-ko` and `antislop-en`, each with a copy editor and a chief editor the `review` check runs. Screens, slides, cards and posters have the same disease and no pack: the purple-gradient hero over three rounded feature cards, glass panels, emoji as icons, "Welcome back 👋", stat tiles with fake numbers, fade-up on every section and hover-lift on every card, one typeface for everything, everything centred, copy that says "seamlessly" and "unlock". Anthropic's own `frontend-design` skill names the same clusters and prescribes a design plan before code; vibe adds what a skill cannot — an independent verdict the writing model cannot give itself.

A pack is a medium, not only a language. The `review` check therefore learns `pack` (the language packs keep working through `lang`), reviewer stages come from the pack's directory in numbered order instead of two hard-coded names, and the Codex side is proven live, not assumed. The two things worth borrowing from fluent-korean go into `antislop-ko` in the same patch.

Ten public anti-slop skills were read whole and compared with the text packs (blader/humanizer, aboudjem/humanizer-skill, petergyang/no-ai-slop, ehmo/slopkit, elithrar/anti-slop, aashaexo/soundshuman, cursor/unslop, stephenturner/deslop, jalaalrd/anti-ai-slop-writing, hardikpandya/stop-slop). All ten are English prose only; none has an independent verdict, a genre frame, a preservation contract or a Korean pack. Their better ideas are precision devices the packs lack: strength tiers so a weak tell alone is not acted on, five questions before a cut, the portability test, the writer's notes inside a source kept out of the output, a voice sample that outranks the rules, and guardrails against overcorrecting a low-variance or non-native writer. Those go in; the absolute bans (every em dash, every adverb, "two beats three") do not.

## What counts as success
- `skills/antislop-design/SKILL.md` (English, ≤ 600 lines) is a design pack in the shape of the text packs: what design slop is and how to see it (a catalogue of the generic clusters, with the markers visible in markup and CSS), the decisions a specific design makes first (who uses it and how often, the task, density, hierarchy, one or two typefaces with a reason, colour from the brand or the content as four to six named tokens, structure that encodes information, motion only with a purpose, the empty · loading · error · long-text · many-items states, contrast and keyboard access), the four tasks (write: design plan — tokens, type, layout sketch — before code; diagnose: findings table, nothing changed; revise: preservation contract for function, data, accessibility and brand tokens; convert: template → specific), a verification checklist, and the editorial-review section that points at the `review` check with `pack: design`. It covers web UI, slides, card news and posters alike; copy inside a design follows the language pack of that language. `references/catalogue.md` holds the slop catalogue with code-level markers; `references/plan.md` the design-plan template.
- `reviewers/design/1-markup-reviewer.md` and `reviewers/design/2-art-director.md` (each ≤ 300 lines) judge, in that order: the markup reviewer reads the source — semantic structure, accessibility basics, contrast, the slop markers, copy defects — and returns exactly `PASS` or a `REJECT` list in the four-field form the text packs use; the art director judges the rendered result when a screenshot is given (opened with its file tool) and the source otherwise — hierarchy, specificity to the brief and content, states, distinctiveness — same output form. `agents/design-markup-reviewer.md` and `agents/design-art-director.md` carry the same bodies for Claude Code; the Codex TOML agents are generated from the reviewer files as before.
- Reviewer stages are data: every `reviewers/<pack>/` holds `N-<stage>.md` files and the `review` check runs them in numeric order, naming each `<pack> <stage>: PASS`. The text packs move to `1-copy-editor.md` / `2-chief-editor.md`; agents and Codex TOML keep their names (`ko-copy-editor`, …).
- The `review` check gains `pack` (`ko` · `en` · `design` · any directory under `reviewers/`); `lang` stays as the text-pack alias; with neither, the language of the manuscript chooses a text pack as today. For `pack: design`, `path` may be a file or a directory (then every `.html .htm .css .js .jsx .ts .tsx .vue .svelte .svg` under it, sorted, up to the 400,000-character cap), the source goes to the reviewers numbered line by line under `## Source`, and an optional `screenshot` path goes under `## Screenshot` with the instruction to open it. No language detection for a non-text pack.
- `vibe-scope` proposes `check: { type: review, pack: design, path: <file or dir>, contract: <brief>, screenshot?: <png> }` marked `⚠ model-judged` when a success condition is a screen, page, component, dashboard, slide, card or poster; the six common skills stay ≤ 300 lines.
- `antislop-ko` gains, in its punctuation section, the rule that an em dash which hides the relation between its two sides becomes a colon, a conjunction or two sentences; and, in the sentence-skeleton section, a `### 조사·어미 생략` subsection with four before → after pairs showing meaning lost by a dropped particle or ending. Both are written fresh in the pack's own voice; the pack stays ≤ 600 lines.
- `antislop-en` and `antislop-ko` each gain six short sections, written in the pack's own voice and register: `### Strength tiers` / `### 표지의 강도` (strong tells act alone; weak tells act only when two or more overlap in one passage); `### Five questions before a cut` / `### 자르기 전 다섯 질문` (does meaning go with it · is it the writer's choice or a form · does it deliver or declare · is it heat or light · does a sceptical reader trust the sentence more without it); `### Portability test` / `### 이식 시험` (a sentence that moves unchanged to another person, company, country or product is filler); `### Notes to the writer are not content` / `### 원문 속 지시문` (an instruction mixed into the source — "keep this tone", "shorter here" — is executed, never reprinted); `### A sample outranks the rules` / `### 필자 샘플이 규칙보다 앞선다` (with a sample of the author's own prose, match its rhythm and its dash rate even where the pack would cut); `### What is not a tell` / `### 표지가 아닌 것` (quotations, proper nouns, text written before November 2022, a low-variance or non-native writer's precision, a real hedge that states real uncertainty). The English catalogue also names false range, the "serves as" dodge, the magic adverb, "Not X. Not Y. Just Z.", the bold-first bullet, the colon reveal, engineering metaphor nouns (substrate, flywheel, north star, wedge, vector) and "say what it does, not how it feels"; the Korean catalogue names the counterparts that exist in Korean. Each pack stays ≤ 600 lines.
- All four text reviewers (`ko` and `en`, both stages) apply the overlap rule — a weak tell alone is not a REJECT — and may end a REJECT list with up to three `KEEP | <span> | <reason>` lines naming what the writer must not touch, so a revision does not scrub the voice while fixing the finding. `PASS` stays exactly `PASS`.
- `checks/packs.js` replaces `checks/language-pack.js`: every `skills/antislop-*` has a `reviewers/<pack>/` with at least two numbered stages, sizes within budget, agents in step, no client-only tool names; the workflow and CLAUDE.md name it.
- Tests: the review check with a fake reviewer proves `pack: design` on a file and on a directory (file list, numbered source, screenshot line, stage names and order from the prefixes), that `lang: ko` and detection still choose the text packs and their renamed stages, and that an unknown pack fails with a reason; the plugin test proves the design agents are generated for Codex.
- Live, Claude: the `review` check on `checks/fixtures/slop/index.html` — a page built from the catalogue (gradient hero, three glass feature cards, emoji icons, "Welcome back 👋", stat tiles, fade-up on every section) — fails at the markup reviewer with a REJECT that names at least two catalogue markers.
- Live, Codex: the same fixture through `VIBE_REVIEW_CMD="codex exec --skip-git-repo-check -"` also fails with a REJECT at the markup reviewer; and `vibe read --ask` twice through the Codex reader (`VIBE_READER_CMD` unset, `claude` hidden from PATH) reports the second run as resumed with cache-read tokens above zero.
- Earlier gates still hold: build, tests, card ≤ 1KB, file 400 / function 50, six common skills ≤ 300 lines, packs within budget, plugin tree current for 4.1.11, README status line carries `4.1.11`.

## Constraints
- No new dependency; the harness renders nothing — a screenshot, when wanted, is produced by the project's own tooling in an earlier scenario and handed to the check by path.
- The text packs' behaviour, agent names and Codex TOML names do not change; only the reviewer files move.
- The pack is written in English (its content is design, not a language); copy inside a design follows the language pack of that language.
- Every record is English; the model talks to the user in the user's language.
