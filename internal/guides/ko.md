# Korean writing

Write natural Korean appropriate to the reader and purpose. State the answer first. Use concrete subjects and verbs, familiar words and evidence for numerical claims. Avoid translationese, empty praise, canned conclusions and unnecessary contrast. Use the user's terminology; describe degraded behavior as 품질 저하 and preventive tests as 재발 방지 테스트.

Revise while writing. Do not invoke a copy editor or chief editor by default.
