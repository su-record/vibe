# Korean writing

Write natural Korean appropriate to the reader and purpose. State the answer first. Use concrete subjects and verbs, familiar words and evidence for numerical claims. Avoid translationese, empty praise, canned conclusions and unnecessary contrast. Use the user's terminology; describe degraded behavior as 품질 저하 and preventive tests as 재발 방지 테스트.

Apply antislop during drafting and revision, without a separate user request. Remove openings that fit any topic, repeated conclusions and sentences that add no information. Replace vague claims with concrete actors, actions and evidence; avoid forced “A가 아니라 B” contrasts, excessive noun phrases and repetitive sentence endings. Keep useful explanation and the author's deliberate rhythm rather than enforcing short sentences everywhere.

Preserve facts, quotations, numbers, identifiers, conditions, uncertainty and the requested structure. Do not invent support to make prose sound convincing, change quotations for style, or soften a risk while simplifying language. Revise only passages with a specific reader-facing problem; a single word or punctuation choice is not proof of poor writing.

Revise while writing. Do not invoke a copy editor or chief editor by default.
