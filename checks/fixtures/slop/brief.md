# Design brief: ward shift roster

## What this is

A shift-roster screen for a hospital ward. It shows who is on shift now, who
is on call next, and the coming week's schedule.

## Who uses it, and how often

Charge nurses, checked roughly twenty times per shift, for a few seconds at
a time, on a desk monitor at the nurses' station.

## The task

See who is on shift right now and who is on call next, at a glance, without
scrolling or clicking through a menu.

## Constraints

- Must be legible from arm's length across a shared desk monitor.
- No decorative content: every number and name shown must be real roster data.
- High density: the full week should fit on one screen.
- No marketing tone. This is a working tool checked between patients, not a
  product a nurse has chosen to use.

## What "done" looks like

A charge nurse can identify who is on shift and who is on call within two
seconds of looking at the screen, and can read the rest of the week's
roster without scrolling.
